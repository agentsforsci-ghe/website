Before the workshop

Six steps. Steps 1 to 3 are about your own work and your own thinking, and they are the ones the day depends on. Steps 4 to 6 are setup and admin.

Work through them in order. Steps 1 and 2 have no due date because you bring the result with you; the other four have due dates so that problems surface while there is still time to fix them.

Step What Due
1 Bring a piece of your own data bring it with you
2 Write down how you publish a paper bring it with you
3 Read the AI declaration questionnaires

Friday, 26 September 2026
4 Install Claude Code before Step 5
5 Test your setup

Monday, 22 September 2026
6 Complete the pre-course survey

Monday, 15 September 2026
TipHow long will this take?

Steps 1 and 2 are thinking, not typing: half an hour each if you already know which dataset you want to use. Step 3 is about an hour of reading. Steps 4 to 6 are around half an hour together, unless something goes wrong with the install, which is exactly why Step 5 has a due date a week before the workshop.

ImportantIf you get stuck

Do not spend an evening fighting an install. Open an issue on your setup repository (Step 5 explains how) or email lschoebitz@ethz.ch. Getting you unstuck before the day is the whole point of having pre-work.
